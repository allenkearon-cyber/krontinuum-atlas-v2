EVIDENCE CLASSES

PProved. Exact algebraic consequence or proof mechanism. Classical ingredients are named as classical and not claimed as new. CCertified. Exhaustive over a stated finite universe, with archived scripts, manifests and completion flags. EEmpirical. Observed repeatedly on a stated sample or grid. Not a theorem. NNegative. Every plate in this atlas carries its class in the notes. A framework that produced exact structure everywhere would be unfalsifiable, so the failures are drawn at the same weight as the successes.

PLATE AND PRINT

The two modes are not a recolour. As an illuminated plate the bodies emit and accumulate, and halation spreads from the brightest of them. Explanatory page

The families

Four directions on a nine-point plane. Everything found after them turns out to be indexed by those same four, which is why this page exists: to say plainly what a family is here, and what keeps reappearing.

A family in this programme is not a metaphor for a grouping. It is a set of objects that some exact symmetry permutes among themselves and does not mix with anything else. Each one below was recovered from frozen data by a separate audit, and the reason they are collected on one page is that they keep turning out to be the same four things wearing different clothes.

The starting point is the affine plane AG(2,3): nine cells, twelve lines, four parallel classes of three. Those four parallel classes are the four direction families. Two of them came from the scaffold and curvature branches; the other two came from the frozen v040 block configuration. They were not designed to agree.

The labels generator and polar are relative to whichever kernel was selected, not properties of the directions. G019 showed that no pair is globally privileged: all six choices exist and form one orbit. The programme's frozen selection is one ordered polarization inside a larger family of three.

2 · SIX CYCLE-MODULE FAMILIES

Pick any two of the four directions. Their six lines carry a parity map, and that map is exactly the binary boundary map of K3,3 — the nine base cells are its edges, the six lines its vertices. The kernel is the cycle space, so its dimension is 9 − 6 + 1 = 4 and it holds one zero state, nine 4-cycles and six Hamiltonian 6-cycles.

Six pairs give six modules: XY, XP, XM, YP, YM, PM. Each module's q = 1 states split into two triangle components, and here is the rule worth remembering — they are labelled by the two directions the module did not use. The directions that build the boundary map are absent from the triangle labels, and the two omitted directions reappear as the two components. This complement rule holds for all six.

3 · THREE POLARIZATION FAMILIES

Opposite pairs among four directions give three ways to split the six modules in half: XY | PM, XP | YM, XM | YP. In each case the two 4-dimensional modules are mutually orthogonal, meet only at zero, and sum to the same 8-dimensional plus-type binary space of 256 states.

4 · TWO NORM FAMILIES

Once a polarization is chosen, the four directions split 2 + 2 under the anisotropic ternary quadratic form: two of norm one, two of norm two. This is the same split as norm-one against norm-two elements of F9*, and it is what explains two earlier results at once — the direction orbits of size 2 that broke the predicted transitivity, and the exchange of the two binary halves. The orthogonal group preserves the two families; the multiplier-two coset swaps them.

5 · FOUR 30-STATE ROOT-PAIR FAMILIES

The four direction coordinates generate the ternary tetracode. All eight of its nonzero words have weight three, so each vanishes in exactly one coordinate, and they fall into four projective ± pairs — one per direction. Gluing four A2 root systems along that code gives an even unimodular rank-8 lattice with 240 roots: E8. Reducing modulo 2E8 collapses the 240 roots to 120 ± classes, and those are exactly the 120 nonsingular states of the binary module.

WHAT THIS PAGE IS NOT CLAIMING

AG(2,3), K3,3, plus-type quadratic forms, Hamming graphs, F9, the tetracode, A2 and E8 are all classical, long-understood objects. None of them is being claimed as new. What is specific to this programme is the route: each was recovered independently from frozen descent, closure and fusion data, by separate audits that did not know about each other, and only afterwards found to assemble along one set of four directions.

Two identifications were tested and refused. A group of order 1,152 appearing on both sides is not an isomorphism — the element-order spectra differ, so W(F4) is rejected. And the count 66 appears in two different lower-family domains without any bijection between them, so they are kept separate. Matching cardinality is not evidence, and the record treats it as a question rather than an answer.
