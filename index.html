PLATE AND PRINT

The two modes are not a recolour. As an illuminated plate the bodies emit and accumulate, and halation spreads from the brightest of them. As a paper print the same geometry is laid down as ink: the marks multiply into the paper and absorb light out of it, so density reads where emission read before. Depth is carried by occlusion in both — a near body hides what sits behind it rather than adding to it.
